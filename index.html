Bhutan Tourism Hub

Bhutan Tourism Hub is a Bhutan-based platform for discovering and booking verified Bhutanese tour guides, drivers, tour operators and hotels. Every guide and driver carries a Department of Tourism licence that the hub checks. Guest reviews are tied to real trips, so a rating means a trip actually happened.

For tour operators

  • Find licensed guides and drivers by language, skill and availability, and send a booking request with the dates filled in.
  • Plan the itinerary day by day with Drukpah, the hub's route planner for Bhutan's real roads and passes.
  • Request hotel rooms night by night; hotels confirm with one tap and can never be double-booked.
  • Keep the trip's checklist, guests, crew and chat in one place.

For guides and drivers

  • Receive direct requests from operators; accepting one protects those dates on your calendar.
  • Get a brief for each trip: the flight, the route, the guests' needs, where the group sleeps.
  • Build a record that travels with you: verified reviews from guests and past trips attested by operators.

For hotels

  • List room types once; receive room requests from operators and confirm or decline in one tap.
  • Post rooms, views and seasonal offers to every operator on the hub.

Free for licensed guides, drivers, tour operators and hotels in Bhutan. No commission.
