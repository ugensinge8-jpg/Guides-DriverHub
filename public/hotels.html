What your property gets

  • Requests with the details filled in — the nights, the rooms and who's asking.
  • One-tap answers — confirm or decline; the operator hears straight away, even with the app closed.
  • No double-booking — confirmed nights are held for that trip.
  • Your offers in front of every operator — post rooms, views and seasonal offers to the hub.
  • Hotels, resorts, farmstays and homestays, listed by town and kind of stay.

How a room request works

  1. An operator plans a trip Their itinerary shows which town the group sleeps in each night.
  2. They request your rooms For the nights they need, with the number and type of rooms.
  3. You answer Confirm or decline from your phone; the operator is told at once, and confirmed nights are held for that trip.
  4. Changes are clear If an operator withdraws a request, or you cancel a booking, the other side hears about it — nothing is left hanging.

What operators see about your property

Your offers
