Questions

Is there a cost for tour operators?

No. Bhutan Tourism Hub is free for tour operators in Bhutan and takes no commission on bookings.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. It runs in the browser on any phone or computer and can be added to the home screen like an app, including on iPhone.

Can I keep working without a connection?

Yes. The app opens on what's saved on your phone and shows your last notifications; saving changes needs a connection.

How do guest reviews work?

After a trip you send each guest a one-time link. They review the guide and the driver separately, and you publish or hide each review before it appears on the person's profile.
