The right crew for VIP and private trips in Bhutan

A VIP trip is only as good as the people with the guests every day. Bhutan Tourism Hub helps tour operators choose them on evidence: licences, real guests' reviews and how other operators graded them.

Choosing a crew for discerning guests

  • Track record, not promises — averages of operators' grades for punctuality, conduct, presentation, guest care, knowledge or safe driving, and communication.
  • Reviews from real guests — each one tied to an actual trip and confirmed by its operator.
  • Languages and specialities — filter for the language your guests speak and the trip's focus: culture, trekking, wellness, photography, birding.
  • Discretion built in — the trip's details, guest list and chat are shared only with its crew and operator.
  • Hotels by tier — request rooms night by night from properties listed by town, kind of stay and star rating.

Travellers planning a VIP or private trip book it with a licensed Bhutanese tour operator, who arranges the guide, driver and stays. The hub is the operators' tool for choosing them well; it doesn't sell trips to travellers directly.

Questions

How do I arrange a VIP trip to Bhutan?

Book with a licensed Bhutanese tour operator. Operators on Bhutan Tourism Hub choose senior guides and experienced drivers using guest reviews, other operators' grades, languages and specialities, and request rooms from hotels on the hub.

Can a private trip have the same guide throughout?

Yes. A guide's confirmed dates are protected on their calendar for your trip, so they can't be booked elsewhere.
