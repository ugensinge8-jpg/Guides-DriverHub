Bhutan Tourism Hub

A trip, start to finish.
Ninety seconds.

How a Bhutanese tour operator runs a trip on the hub — from the first enquiry to the guide's brief. Real screens, not mock-ups.

No one reaches it alone

A tour operator opens the hub on any phone. Nothing to install.

1 · The enquiry comes in

Every enquiry in one pipeline: new, quoted, won, lost. The hub reminds you who to follow up and when.

2 · The trip takes shape

Confirm it and the trip has a page: guests, with ages and nationalities for the SDF and visa; crew; the day plan.

3 · Crew, in one tap

Invite a guide or driver already on the hub, or send a new one a link on WhatsApp. They accept from their phone.

4 · Drukpah drafts the itinerary

Describe the trip in a few lines. Drukpah builds the day-by-day plan on Bhutan's real roads, places your signature experiences, and works out the SDF.

5 · The plan goes on the trip

One tap puts Drukpah's days onto the trip, ready to edit.

6 · The crew get their brief

Your guide sees the flight, the guests, the dietary notes and the plan on his phone. No more WhatsApp chaos.

7 · You see the business

Trips, guests and nights against last year, where guests come from, why enquiries are lost, and what to do about it.
