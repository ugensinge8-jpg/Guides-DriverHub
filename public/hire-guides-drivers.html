  3. Request Send a job request with the dates filled in; they accept or decline in one tap.

  5. Run the trip The trip page keeps the crew, guests, itinerary, hotel rooms, checklist and chat together.

Questions

How do I hire a freelance guide in Bhutan?

Sign in as a tour operator on Bhutan Tourism Hub, open Find talent, filter by language, speciality and free dates, and send a job request with your trip dates. The guide accepts or declines in one tap, and the dates are protected on their calendar.

Are the guides and drivers licensed?

Each guide and driver uploads their Department of Tourism licence. Profiles show a Verified tick once the licence has been checked.

How do I know if a guide is good?

Profiles show reviews from real guests (sent through a one-time link tied to the trip and confirmed by its operator) and averages of the grades other tour operators gave after trips — punctuality, conduct, cleanliness, guest care, knowledge or safe driving, and communication.

Does Bhutan Tourism Hub charge a commission?

No. The hub is free for licensed guides, drivers, tour operators and hotels in Bhutan, and takes no commission.

Can travellers book a guide directly here?

Not at the moment. The hub is for Bhutan's tour operators and the guides, drivers and hotels they work with. Travellers book their trip with a licensed tour operator, who arranges the crew.
