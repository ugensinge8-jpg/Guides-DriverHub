Getting started

Ten minutes, no fee, no commission.

You keep every ngultrum. The hub takes nothing from operators or guides.

Step 1

Create your account

Name, phone, home base and languages. Two minutes on any phone; no app store needed.

Step 2

Upload your DoT licence

A photo is enough. Once checked, your profile carries the Verified tick that operators look for first.

Step 3

Set your calendar

Block the days you can't work. From then on, requests arrive only for days you're free.
